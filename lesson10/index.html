About Me

I’m a web development student at San Diego Mesa College with a strong interest in front-end development, user experience design, and accessibility. I enjoy building interfaces that are visually engaging, thoughtfully designed, and usable by as many people as possible.

My background combines technical coursework with creative problem-solving. I’m especially drawn to projects that require balancing aesthetics, usability, and accessibility—making sure the experience works just as well with a keyboard or screen reader as it does with a mouse.

I believe accessibility and good design go hand in hand. When accessibility is built into a project from the start, the result is a cleaner, more intuitive experience for everyone.

I approach web development with an emphasis on semantic HTML, responsive layouts, and thoughtful interaction design. I’m intentional about how users move through a page and how different people might interact with the same interface in different ways.
