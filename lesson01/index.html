About Me

Hey, I’m Jessica. I’m currently studying Web Development at San Diego Mesa College and learning how to build websites by hand using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I love creating pages that feel expressive, nostalgic, and personal instead of overly polished.

Some of my biggest interests are web design, gaming, interior styling, old internet aesthetics, and anything that blends creativity with technical skill. I wanted this page to feel like an original MySpace profile from back when the internet had way more personality.

My goals are to become a strong front-end developer, keep growing as a designer, and build sites that are both functional and memorable.
